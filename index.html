Returns & Exchange Policy

Exchange or return

You may exchange or return an item within days of delivery, provided it is unworn, unwashed and unaltered, with original tags, packaging and proof of purchase.

Faulty or not as described

If an item arrives damaged, defective or different from its description, we will replace it or give you a full refund — including delivery charges — in line with Qatar’s Consumer Protection Law.

Items that cannot be returned

For hygiene reasons, lingerie, swimwear and face veils cannot be returned, nor can items made-to-measure or altered at your request — unless they are faulty.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within working days of receiving the returned item.
